## Finding category labels

Use the narrowest label:

- `PROMISE` — false, unclear, competing, or dropped reader expectation.
- `PROGRESS` — missing signpost, repetition, stagnation, or unearned leap.
- `PAYOFF` — absent, unprepared, costless, wrong-scale, or thematically disconnected resolution.
- `BEAT` — expected outline beat is partial, missing, or altered.
- `SCENE` — entry/exit state does not change or the scene lacks a functional turn.
- `ESCALATION` — attempt ladder repeats or does not become harder/costlier/smarter.
- `MECHANIC` — solution violates understanding, setup, cost, or limitation.
- `HANDOFF` — ending fails to create the required state or next-chapter pull.
- `CHARACTER` — choices, values, proactivity, relatability, capability, or arc movement are missing or disconnected.
- `WORLDBUILDING` — an element is disconnected, over-explained, under-grounded, or added where expansion would serve better.
- `PROGRESSION` — advancement does not alter options, tactics, identity, pressure, or cost.

## Actionability rules

- For a local issue, quote the exact original span and supply an exact replacement.
- For missing material, quote the seam around the gap and provide the same seam with a drafted insertion when practical.
- For a structural scene issue, state a transformation rule, identify the full span, and draft a worked example. Do not pretend one paragraph replacement fixes an entire scene.
- For outline, arc, thread, or whole-book issues, provide an exact beat insertion, deletion, reorder, split, merge, or transformation rule and name the required resulting state. Do not draft prose unless requested.
- Keep suggested prose voice-matched but provisional. This is structural drafting support, not a final prose pass.
- When a fix would alter canon or a later outline beat, provide options under **Decision Notes** instead of presenting one as approved.
