# Editing Workflow

## Approval

Presenting or saving a proposed rewrite is not permission to apply it. Wait for an explicit instruction such as "make that swap" before changing manuscript prose. The exception is an explicitly requested `chapter-cycle`, which is authorized to complete its one-shot draft, review, regression, and fix loop.

## Applied edits

- Preserve the smallest practical diff; do not improve unrelated prose while applying a targeted change.
- Append every applied manuscript edit, including a one-paragraph change, to that chapter's `drafts/Changelog.md`. Never overwrite earlier entries.
- If an applied edit came from a saved review report, mark the corresponding finding applied and link it to the chapter changelog.
- Recheck chapter and scene openings after moving a scene break, changing POV, or editing an opening sentence; `Writing Style.md`'s POV-opening rule (if you have one) still applies.

## Canon and codex

- A cross-chapter contradiction is a canon decision. Show the established versions with chapter citations and ask which is correct before changing either one. Once decided, update every affected chapter and add matching, cross-linked changelog entries.
- Update a relevant `<Name> - Voice.md` or `codex/plot/Motifs.md` when an edit changes an established voice trait or recurring detail.
- The manuscript is factual record; outlines and planned summaries are predictions. Update chapter frontmatter, summaries, indexes, and codex status to reflect what actually lands on the page.
- Anything drawn from `00 Braindump.md` but not yet written must remain visibly marked as future or unrevealed material.

## Chapter creation

Chapter creation has two stages:

1. Plan: create the folder, frontmatter, `Outline.md`, and future-tense planned `Summary.md`, without prose.
2. Draft: write prose only in a separate explicit pass, then rewrite the summary from what actually appeared.

Before outlining or drafting, read the previous chapter's summary, `Genre.md`, `Writing Style.md`, and the relevant character, location, mechanic, plot, and voice notes. Use `00 Index.md` and `codex/plot/Macro Progression.md` for direction when instructions are thin.

## Reviews

- Save review reports in the vault-level `reviews/` folder, one report per chapter. Chapter `drafts/` folders are for versions and changelogs.
- Actionable findings need an exact original anchor and a concrete replacement. Structural findings need a transformation rule and a worked example.
- A review reports suggestions; `draft-from-review` applies approved findings. The `chapter-cycle` exception may consolidate and apply its reviewers' findings within the authorized one-shot loop.
