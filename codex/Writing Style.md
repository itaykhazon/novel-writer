## Register and pacing

- <fill in your book's register — e.g. "epic fantasy, not a uniformly fast thriller" or "tight, propulsive thriller pacing throughout". Action, worldbuilding, and emotional beats may need different speeds; judge pacing against each scene's purpose, not one fixed tempo.>

- Avoid clichés. If a phrase feels readily available, it is probably worn.
- Dialogue must carry characterization, information, or pressure; cut lines that only fill air.
