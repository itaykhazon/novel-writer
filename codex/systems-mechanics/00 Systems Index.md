# Systems Index

> Optional folder. Use it for anything your world runs on that needs documented rules and limits — a magic system, a technology, a progression or ranking system, an institution's rules (a legal system, a tournament structure, a governing charter). Delete this folder if your novel doesn't need it.

One note per mechanic or system, created with `codex/templates/Mechanic Template.md` via `add-to-codex`. `continuity-reviewer` and `sanderson-review` both check that established limitations and costs stay respected — the more precisely a mechanic's rules are written down here, the more useful those checks are.
